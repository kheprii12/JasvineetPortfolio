About

I'm a fourth-year mechanical engineering student at York University, currently on a co-op with Matcor Metal Fabrication as a process engineer, working across quality, welding, and process teams on live production problems.

I grew up in Scarborough, Ontario, the kind of household where a Saturday off meant a trip to the library to chase down whatever I was curious about that week. That habit never really left — I still default to taking things apart, in CAD or otherwise, to see how they work.

Outside of engineering, I sketch, paint, and sculpt. It sounds unrelated, but the process is nearly identical: plan carefully, adapt when something doesn't go to plan, and stay patient with the details until the whole thing holds together.
